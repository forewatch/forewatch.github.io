Forewatch

A voice-first watch-keeper for the helm.

Forewatch is a mobile application for recreational boaters on the Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway. It listens for a wake word, answers questions about tides, bridges, daylight and weather by voice, and speaks timely alerts for bridges, no-wake zones and restricted areas so a captain can keep both hands and both eyes on the water.

The application is in development and sea trials on the Florida ICW. It is not yet available to the public.
